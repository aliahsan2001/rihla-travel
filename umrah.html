Questions families ask

Before you book

Still unsure? Call us on +92 21 3456 7000. We will not rush you.

How do you measure the distance from the Haram?

On foot, from the hotel door to the nearest gate of the Masjid al-Haram, along the route you would actually walk. For the far hotels we also give the shuttle frequency.

Which vaccinations do I need?

A meningitis (ACYW) vaccination certificate, and a polio certificate for travellers from Pakistan. We tell you where to get both in Karachi, and check them before we apply for the visa.

Can women travel without a mahram?

Under current Saudi rules, women over 18 can perform Umrah in an organised group without a mahram. Rules do change, so we confirm them when you book.

How do I pay?

A deposit of Rs 50,000 per person holds your seats. The balance is due 30 days before departure, by bank transfer or at our office.

Can you change the hotel or the nights?

Yes. Any package can be adjusted; we send a revised price in writing, usually the same day.
